## Short Description
Import orders from 5 Indian marketplaces into your Shopify store. Auto-sync inventory, manage all channels from one dashboard.

## Long Description
Running your store on multiple marketplaces? Tired of copying orders manually between Flipkart, Amazon, Nykaa, Myntra, TataCliq and Shopify?

GlobalSupply Marketplace Sync connects your Shopify store with India's top 5 marketplaces, so you can:

**Import Orders Automatically**
- Orders from Flipkart, Amazon, Nykaa, Myntra, and TataCliq appear as Draft Orders in Shopify
- No more manual entry — orders sync in real-time via webhooks
- Track marketplace orders alongside your Shopify orders

**Sync Inventory**
- Push your Shopify inventory levels to all connected marketplaces
- Prevent overselling — when stock changes on Shopify, marketplaces get updated
- Multi-warehouse support for advanced sellers

**Unified Dashboard**
- See all your connected channels at a glance inside Shopify admin
- Configure each marketplace with your API credentials
- One-click sync for orders and inventory

**Why Choose GlobalSupply?**
- Built specifically for Indian sellers
- Supports Flipkart, Amazon, Nykaa, Myntra, TataCliq
- Free tier available — start with 50 orders/month
- No coding required — just enter your marketplace API keys
- Secure — your credentials are encrypted and never shared
