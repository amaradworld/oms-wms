Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 19, 2026 | Last Updated: June 19, 2026

GlobalSupply Techno ("we," "our," or "us") operates the GlobalSupply order management and warehouse management platform (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Service.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you register, we collect your name, email address, company name, phone number, and GSTIN (if applicable).

Order Data: We process order details, customer information, product SKUs, inventory levels, and shipment tracking data that you enter into the platform.

Marketplace Data: When you connect marketplace accounts (Nykaa, Myntra, TataCliq, Amazon, Flipkart, Shopify), we receive order and inventory sync data via authorized API integrations.

Usage Data: We collect log data including IP address, browser type, pages visited, time spent, and interaction patterns within the Service.

Device Information: We may collect device type, operating system, and unique device identifiers for barcode scanning features.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • To process orders and sync inventory across connected marketplaces
  • To send transactional emails (order confirmations, password resets, alerts)
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • To comply with legal obligations including GST and tax regulations
  • To communicate service updates and promotional messages (with opt-out)

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data with:

  • Marketplace Partners: Nykaa, Myntra, TataCliq, Amazon, Flipkart, Shopify — only the data necessary for order and inventory sync
  • Payment Processers: Razorpay — for payment processing (subject to their privacy policy)
  • Infrastructure Providers: Vercel, Render, Supabase, Cloudflare — for hosting and delivery
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law, court order, or government regulation

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encryption in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest
  • JWT-based authentication with secure token storage
  • API key authentication with timing-safe comparison
  • HMAC-SHA256 webhook signature verification
  • Rate limiting to prevent abuse
  • Role-based access control (RBAC) for multi-user accounts

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your personal data within 30 days, except where required by law (e.g., GST records must be retained for 6 years).

6. Your Rights

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and personal data
  • Export: Export your data in standard formats (CSV, JSON)
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from promotional communications

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. We do not use third-party advertising cookies.

8. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. International Data Transfers

Your data is stored on servers in India and may be processed in countries where our infrastructure providers operate. By using the Service, you consent to such transfers.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes via email or in-app notification.
