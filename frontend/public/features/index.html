Order Management

From cart to doorstep, one inbox

All your orders from every channel land in a single unified inbox. No more juggling Shopify, Nykaa seller, and Amazon tabs. Triage, edit, split, cancel — and bulk-process hundreds in seconds.

  • Unified inbox across Shopify, WooCommerce, Nykaa, Myntra, TataCliq, Amazon, Flipkart
  • Bulk actions: pick, pack, dispatch, print, cancel
  • Split orders across warehouses by SKU availability or pincode
  • Edit orders post-purchase with full audit trail
  • Custom order tags, filters, saved views, CSV export
  • Auto-cancellation for COD risk orders via configurable rules
Inventory & Warehousing

One source of truth for every SKU

Track stock at the bin, shelf, or facility level. Sync inventory in real-time to every sales channel. Stop overselling. Stop stockouts. Stop reconciling spreadsheets at midnight.

  • Multi-warehouse, multi-facility hierarchy
  • Bin-level tracking with EPC/RFID barcode support
  • Batch, lot, MRP, expiry date tracking (FMCG-ready)
  • Real-time stock sync to Shopify, Nykaa, Amazon, Myntra
  • Low-stock alerts and reorder-point automation
  • Stock transfers between warehouses with in-transit visibility
Picking & Packing

Mobile-first workflows for the warehouse floor

Pickers and packers spend their day on their feet. We designed the picking app for one-handed, glove-on, barcode-scanner use — not for managers at desks.

  • Wave-based picking: group orders by route, courier, or priority
  • Short-pick handling with reason codes and reorder suggestions
  • Packing station with courier auto-allocation by pincode & weight
  • Thermal label printing (4×6 shipping labels, barcoded)
  • Manifest generation in courier-specific formats
  • Works on any phone browser — no app install required
