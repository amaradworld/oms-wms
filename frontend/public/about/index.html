About us

Transforming Supply Chains Through Technology

GlobalSupply was founded with a simple mission: to simplify warehouse, inventory, and order management for growing businesses.

Our Story

After years of working in supply chain operations, warehousing, inventory control, and e-commerce fulfillment, we experienced firsthand the challenges businesses face every day — inventory mismatches, delayed dispatches, stock visibility issues, manual processes, and disconnected systems.

Most software solutions in the market were either too complex, too expensive, or built by teams that had never managed a warehouse floor themselves.

GlobalSupply was created to bridge that gap.

Our cloud-based Order Management System (OMS) and Warehouse Management System (WMS) combines operational expertise with modern technology to help businesses manage inventory, orders, warehouses, logistics, returns, and fulfillment from a single platform.

Whether you are a D2C brand, retailer, distributor, wholesaler, or third-party logistics provider, GlobalSupply helps you streamline operations, improve inventory accuracy, reduce manual effort, and scale with confidence.

Meet the Founder

Alok Garg

Founder & CEO, GlobalSupply

Alok Garg is a supply chain and warehouse operations professional with more than 8 years of experience managing complex logistics, inventory, fulfillment, reverse logistics, and warehouse operations across retail and e-commerce environments.

Throughout his career, he has worked closely with warehouse teams, inventory managers, operations leaders, logistics partners, and technology platforms, gaining deep insight into the operational challenges that businesses face every day.

Having managed inventory discrepancies, fulfillment bottlenecks, warehouse process improvements, reverse logistics operations, cycle counting programs, and large-scale operational projects, Alok recognized a recurring problem: businesses were struggling with fragmented systems and inefficient processes that limited growth.

This realization led to the creation of GlobalSupply.

His vision was to build a platform that combines practical operational expertise with modern cloud technology, enabling businesses to manage their entire supply chain ecosystem from a single, intelligent platform.

Today, Alok is focused on building GlobalSupply into a global technology company serving D2C brands, retailers, distributors, wholesalers, and logistics providers across multiple markets.

His mission is simple: help businesses operate smarter, scale faster, and deliver better customer experiences through technology-driven supply chain excellence.
