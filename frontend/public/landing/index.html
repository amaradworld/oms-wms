What's inside

One platform, every step of the order lifecycle

From the moment an order lands to the courier pickup at your dock — SupplyHub keeps your inventory, people, and parcels in sync.

Order management

Centralize orders from every channel. Auto-route, split, and prioritize with rules. SLA timers and escalations built in.

Bin-level inventory

Track every SKU across facilities, aisles, racks, and bins. Reorder points, batch/expiry, and real-time reservations.

Wave picking

Group orders into waves, route pickers, and scan-to-confirm. Auto-handle short picks and partial fulfilments.

Packing station

Scan-to-pack, multi-SKU boxes, courier allocation rules by weight/zone, and instant invoice + shipping label.

3PL ready

Multi-tenant by design. Onboard client warehouses, allocate inventory, share branded tracking, and invoice each tenant separately.

Real-time analytics

Live dashboards for orders, picker productivity, NDR rates, and revenue. Exportable reports for finance and ops.

How it works

Live in 30 minutes, not 30 days

No consultants, no six-week implementations. Plug in, configure, and start shipping.

1

Connect your channels

One-click OAuth with Nykaa, Myntra, TataCliq, Shopify, Amazon, and Flipkart. Or push orders via REST API.

2

Map your warehouse

Add facilities, bins, and SKUs via CSV upload. Set reorder points and courier routing rules in minutes.

3

Start shipping

Orders flow in, pickers scan, packers pack, couriers collect. Watch the dashboard fill up in real time.
