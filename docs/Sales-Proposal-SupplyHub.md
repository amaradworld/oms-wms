## Slide 5 — Inbound: PO to Putaway

**On slide:**
- Title: "Inbound: From PO to Putaway, Without a Spreadsheet"
- Flow: PO → ASN → GRN (with barcode scan + QC) → Putaway → Bin Location
- Callouts:
  - Vendor Invoice No. tracking for GST compliance
  - QC station with Pass/Fail scan
  - Multi-source putaway (GRN, Gatepass, Returns, Stock Transfer)

**Speaker script:**
> Let's walk through what actually happens in your warehouse today, and how SupplyHub changes it. When you raise a Purchase Order to a supplier, we generate a PO with expected quantities and dates. The supplier sends an Advance Shipping Notice — ASN — so your team knows what's coming. When the truck arrives, you open the GRN — Goods Receipt Note — and the system walks you through scanning each item, marking QC pass or fail, and capturing batch numbers, expiry, MRP, and the vendor invoice number for your GST records. Approved stock moves to a 'GRN Received' staging bin, and one click generates putaway tasks. Your floor staff uses a mobile scanner or a desktop scan page to put away each item to its assigned bin. The entire journey — PO to bin — is tracked, timestamped, and auditable. No paper, no WhatsApp, no missed items.

## Slide 7 — Outbound: Order to Shipment in 90 Seconds

**On slide:**
- Title: "Outbound: From Order to Shipment, in 90 Seconds"
- Flow: Order → Wave Picking → Packing Station → Manifest → Courier Handover
- Callouts:
  - Wave Picking groups orders for batch efficiency
  - Per-SKU scan count (not bulk) — catches short picks
  - One-scan receive + QC at GRN
  - Uniware-style tax invoices & shipping labels (auto-generated)

**Speaker script:**
> Outbound is where your revenue is realized, and this is where most systems slow down. SupplyHub uses Wave Picking — you group 20-30 orders into a wave, your picker walks a single optimized path through the warehouse, and scans each SKU. Crucially, the system tracks scanned quantity vs. ordered quantity, per SKU — so if you order 2 units and only 1 is on the shelf, the system tells you immediately. You can then choose to process the order with a short pick, or hold it for replenishment. At the packing station, the packer scans each item — no more 'is this the right one?' — and the system prints a GST-compliant tax invoice and a shipping label in Uniware format. Same format, every time. Finally, you generate a manifest — a single PDF that lists every shipment going to a courier — and hand it over to Delhivery or BlueDart with one click. Order to shipment in under 90 seconds.

## Pitch Delivery Tips

1. **Open with empathy, not features.** Slides 2-3 are about the client's pain. Spend 5 minutes here.
2. **Show, don't tell.** After the pitch, do a live demo with sample data. Have the warehouse flow open in one tab, dashboard in another.
3. **Bring printed ROI numbers** for the prospect's specific volume — slide 12 with their numbers is your strongest closer.
4. **Anchor on Uniware pricing.** Their per-order fees are public knowledge; clients will know the math.
5. **Don't oversell.** Mention "we don't have a mobile native app yet" honestly — it builds trust for the rest.
6. **Always end with the trial CTA.** Free trials convert 3x better than demos in this market.
